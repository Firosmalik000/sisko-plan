# Phase Registry

A short command such as `Phase 2` maps to the corresponding file below.

| Phase | Name | Primary outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Product Specification and Foundation | Repository and product rules are ready |
| 1 | Identity, Platform Admin, and Multi-Store | Secure login and isolated stores |
| 2 | Master Data | Store can configure products and operational references |
| 3 | Inventory, Cash, and Capital | Correct ledgers and opening balances |
| 4 | Purchasing and Supplier Payable | Purchases increase stock and track payment/debt |
| 5 | POS, Sales, COGS, and Returns | Store can sell through a real POS flow |
| 6 | Expenses, Dashboard, and Reports | Owner can understand business position |
| 7 | Subscription and Platform Commercial Management | SaaS operator can commercialize and control usage |
| 8 | Hardening, Pilot, and Production Readiness | Safe pilot-ready Stage 1 release |

The detailed scope and acceptance criteria live in `docs/phases/PHASE-N.md`.
