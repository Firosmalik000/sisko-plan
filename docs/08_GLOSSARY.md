# Glossary

## Terms

- **Business**: The tenant, ownership, subscription, and plan-limit boundary.
- **Business Membership**: A person's role and lifecycle inside one Business; it may be linked to a User or be POS-only.
- **Owner**: Business member with ultimate control and ownership-recovery protection.
- **Admin**: Business-wide manager who can operate Stores and manage staff within policy.
- **Manager**: Staff assigned to selected Stores with operational oversight and approval capability.
- **Cashier**: Staff assigned to selected Stores for register and sale work.
- **Platform admin**: SaaS operator with cross-platform administrative access.
- **Store**: A Business location and operational ledger boundary.
- **Active store**: The store currently selected by the authenticated user.
- **Master data**: Reference data such as products, categories, units, suppliers, and accounts.
- **Posted transaction**: A finalized business transaction that should remain auditable.
- **Ledger**: The traceable history of financial or stock changes.
- **Projection**: A fast-read balance table derived from ledger activity.
- **COGS**: Cost of goods sold.
- **POS**: Point of sale.
- **Stage 1**: The initial production scope for the product.
