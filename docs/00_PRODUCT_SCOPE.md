# Product Scope - Sistem Toko Stage 1

## Product statement

Sistem Toko Stage 1 is a multi-country Business-scoped SaaS for Southeast Asian merchants that need product, stock, capital, cash, purchasing, POS sales, expenses, supplier debt, and simple but correct reporting.

## Primary user

The main user is a small shop owner who:

- Manages the store personally.
- May own more than one store.
- Needs a system that is cleaner than manual notes.
- Does not yet need full ERP or full accounting.
- Needs a practical POS flow.
- May operate multiple Businesses and Stores across supported countries.

## Platform operator

The SaaS operator manages:

- User accounts.
- All stores from an administrative surface.
- Plans and subscriptions.
- Usage limits.
- Activation and suspension.
- Platform configuration.
- Audit and technical support.

The operator is not the owner of store operational data.

## Stage 1 outcomes

At the end of Stage 1:

1. An owner can register, create a Business, create Stores, and switch Business or Store.
2. Each store remains isolated.
3. An owner can manage master data.
4. An owner can record cash or inventory capital.
5. An owner can record cash, partial, or credit purchases.
6. Purchases update stock and weighted average cost.
7. An owner can make sales through POS.
8. Sales update stock, cash, COGS, and gross profit.
9. An owner can record expenses and cash transfers.
10. An owner can see simple reports.
11. A platform admin can manage the platform and subscriptions.
12. The system has audit, tests, backup readiness, and baseline security.

## Non-goals

Stage 1 is not:

- Full double-entry accounting software.
- ERP.
- Manufacturing software.
- Enterprise procurement software.
- Payroll software.
- Multi-country software.
- Omnichannel marketplace software.
- Native mobile app.
- Offline-first POS.

Multi-country identity, currencies, country-specific payment references, and marketplace clearing are in scope. Supplier marketplace, restaurant, laundry, service-item, payroll, and offline-first vertical workflows remain later specifications.

## Success principles

- Simple for the user.
- Correct in the ledger.
- Secure between stores.
- Testable.
- Extensible without speculative complexity.
